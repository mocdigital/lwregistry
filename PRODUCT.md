# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is an SBO applicant or manager completing a registration, payment, document, and verification journey across desktop and mobile web. The secondary audience is Registry staff processing approvals, payment reviews, compliance documents, support, and procurement operations. The public audience searches the verified directory, reviews business profiles, and submits enquiries.

## Product Purpose

Loveworld Registry gives members and organizations a trusted, structured way to register businesses, complete validation, discover verified organizations, and participate in procurement opportunities. Success means applicants always know what to do next, Registry staff can process work quickly and confidently, and public visitors can trust the information they find.

## Positioning

The Registry should feel like a dependable digital service: clear enough for a first-time applicant, efficient enough for an operations team, and credible enough to support real business decisions.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, composed, helpful, and quietly premium. The interface should reduce anxiety during forms and payments, communicate status plainly, and reserve visual emphasis for decisions and progress.

## Design Principles

1. Guide the next action: every workflow screen should make the current state and next useful step obvious.
2. Make trust visible: show ownership, status, timestamps, verification, payment state, and what happens next.
3. Prefer structure over decoration: use hierarchy, rhythm, and clear surfaces before effects or animation.
4. Make forms forgiving: preserve entered work, explain errors, support mobile input, and distinguish saving from submitting.
5. One system across contexts: public, applicant, and admin screens should share the same semantic components and interaction vocabulary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA practices. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, minimum 44px touch targets, readable contrast, predictable responsive reflow, clear validation, and interfaces that remain usable at 320px wide.
